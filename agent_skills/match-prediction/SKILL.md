# Match Prediction Report

Use only the context snapshot supplied by the Harness. The output is AI judgment, not a guaranteed result or betting advice.

## Workflow

1. Confirm match identity, stage, venue, kickoff, and cutoff.
2. Balance long-term strength, recent form, availability, rest, and tactical evidence.
3. Produce separate 90-minute and, when applicable, qualification probabilities.
4. Include likely scorelines, supporting factors, counter-factors, and unknowns.
5. Review probability sums, source IDs, cutoff integrity, and overconfidence.

## Boundaries

- Do not use information published after the cutoff.
- Do not fabricate lineups, injuries, xG, or ranking values.
- Do not use deterministic or gambling language.
- Freeze the pre-match report at kickoff.
